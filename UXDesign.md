## 1. Project Overview & Concept

* **Title:** Know Your Rights AI Chatbot[cite: 3]
* **What does your AI Chatbot do?**[cite: 3]  
  Explain basic legal concepts in plain language, answer common questions, and connect users to legal aid. The chatbot will have 5 areas of knowledge: Human, Workplace, Consumer, Housing, and Education Rights.[cite: 3]
* **Who is your user? What do they want?**[cite: 3]  
  Our user is the everyday individual who doesn't understand complex legal concepts that they come across on a daily basis. They want to protect their human rights without the financial burden of legal representation.[cite: 3]
* **How do they find your bot?**[cite: 3]  
  The chatbot would be available to anyone with a computer or device that is compatible with Google.[cite: 3]
* **How do they interact with the bot?**[cite: 3]  
  You would ask the bot basic legal questions that can be used as a starting point to solve complex legal cases.[cite: 3]
* **What do they learn?**[cite: 3]  
  They would learn when their basic human rights are being invaded and what legal precautions they can take.[cite: 3]
* **What do they tell others about their experience?**[cite: 3]  
  They would tell others that the chatbot is easy to use and helps explain basic human rights in a clear and understandable way. They would recommend it as a free and helpful starting point for understanding legal issues and finding additional resources.[cite: 3]

## 2. Empathy Map (Pre-Chatbot User)
*Target: Pre-chatbot user attitude in relation to the product, system, or service[cite: 4].*

### Feels (Visceral)[cite: 4]
* Confused or overwhelmed by complicated legal information.[cite: 4]
* Worried about their rights being violated.[cite: 4]
* Frustrated by the cost of legal assistance.[cite: 4]

### Thinks (Reflective)[cite: 4]
* "I don't know if my rights are being violated"[cite: 4]
* "Legal information is too complicated to understand"[cite: 4]
* "There should be an easier way to learn about my rights"[cite: 4]

### Does (Behavioral)[cite: 4]
* Searches the internet for answers to basic legal questions[cite: 4]
* Asks friends or family for advice[cite: 4]
* Avoids seeking legal help because of cost or complexity[cite: 4]

### Says (Communicative)[cite: 4]
* "I don't understand my legal rights"[cite: 4]
* "I can't afford to talk to a lawyer"[cite: 4]
* "I wish there was a free and simple way to get answers"[cite: 4]

### Goals[cite: 4]
* Understanding basic human and legal rights.[cite: 4]
* Finding free, reliable, and easy-to-understand legal information.[cite: 4]
* Knowing what steps to take when their rights are violated.[cite: 4]

## 3. Inclusive Design: User Group Cards (Part 1)
*Directions: 1. Create user group by disability or requirements. 2. Add accommodations/considerations. 3. Consider Overlaps.*[cite: 1]

### 1. Blind[cite: 1]
* **Requirements:** Users need to access information and navigate chatbot without relying on sight.[cite: 1]
* **Accommodations/Considerations:** Provide screen reader compatibility, audio responses, and voice navigation options.[cite: 1]

### 2. Deaf[cite: 1]
* **Requirements:** Users need to understand chatbot information without relying on audio.[cite: 1]
* **Accommodations/Considerations:** Provide written responses, visual notifications, and captions for any audio or video content.[cite: 1]

### 3. Bad Internet[cite: 1]
* **Requirements:** Users need reliable access to basic human rights information even with slow or unstable internet.[cite: 1]
* **Accommodations/Considerations:** Use minimalist design, minimize images and videos, and offer downloadable information for offline use.[cite: 1]

### 4. Illiterate[cite: 1]
* **Requirements:** Users need to understand their basic human rights without having to read complex information.[cite: 1]
* **Accommodations/Considerations:** Provide text to speech, voice commands, and simple visual icons to help users navigate the chatbot.[cite: 1]

### 5. Foreign Language[cite: 2]
* **Requirements:** Users need to understand their human rights in their preferred language without language barriers.[cite: 2]
* **Accommodations/Considerations:** Provide multiple language options and a translation feature so users can easily access and understand information.[cite: 2]

### 6. Underprivileged[cite: 2]
* **Requirements:** Users need free and accessible information about basic human rights without expensive technology or subscriptions.[cite: 2]
* **Accommodations/Considerations:** Make the chatbot free, compatible with older devices, and provide links to free legal aid and community resources.[cite: 2]

### 7. Senior Citizens[cite: 2]
* **Requirements:** Users need an easy-to-navigate chatbot with clear instructions and accessible information.[cite: 2]
* **Accommodations/Considerations:** Include larger text, simple buttons, and a straightforward layout with minimal steps.[cite: 2]

### 8. Students[cite: 2]
* **Requirements:** Students need clear, relevant information about their rights in school, work, and everyday situations.[cite: 2]
* **Accommodations/Considerations:** Provide simple explanations, real-life examples, and easy-to-use interfaces that help students quickly find information relevant to their situations.[cite: 2]
