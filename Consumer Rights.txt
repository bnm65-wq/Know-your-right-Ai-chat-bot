Consumer Rights

Purpose

Our chatbot will help people understand their basic consumer rights using simple language. It will answer common questions and direct users to reliable resources.

1. Refunds and Returns

Consumers may be able to get a refund if an online order never arrives or a seller does not follow its promises. However, stores are not always required to accept returns.

Example Question: My online order never arrived. What can I do?

Chatbot Response: Contact the seller and ask for a refund. If you paid by credit card, you may also be able to dispute the charge.

2. Scams and Fraud

Consumers should be aware of fake websites, suspicious messages, and requests for personal information. Scams can be reported to the Federal Trade Commission (FTC).

Example Question: I think I bought something from a fake website. What should I do?

Chatbot Response: Contact your bank immediately, save any receipts or messages, and report the scam to the FTC.

3. False Advertising

Businesses generally cannot use deceptive advertising to mislead customers about products or services.

Example Question: The product I received is different from what was advertised. What can I do?

Chatbot Response: Save the advertisement and receipt, contact the seller, and report suspected deceptive advertising to the FTC.

4. Billing Disputes

Consumers have protections against incorrect or unauthorized charges. Credit card billing disputes have specific procedures and deadlines.

Example Question: I was charged twice for one purchase. What should I do?

Chatbot Response: Contact your credit card company to report the extra charge and follow its dispute instructions.

Reliable Sources

Federal Trade Commission: https://consumer.ftc.gov/

Report Fraud: https://reportfraud.ftc.gov/

Consumer Financial Protection Bureau: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/

Pennsylvania Attorney General: https://www.attorneygeneral.gov/submit-a-complaint/consumer-en/

Summary

Our chatbot will focus on four common consumer problems: refunds, scams, false advertising, and billing disputes. It will provide simple explanations and direct users to reliable government resources. The chatbot will provide general legal information rather than legal advice.
