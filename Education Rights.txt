Accessibility
    Students have the right to reasonable accomodations. Discrimination based on disability is strictly prohibited. K-12 schools are required to actively identify disabilities while college students have to self identify.
    K-12 schools must provide accomodations for any disability but colleges do not need to change their curriculum.

Privacy
    Students must be given their records within 45 days of requesting them. Corrections can be requested if the student thinks they are wrong or their privacy is violated
    Students must consent before their information is disclosed to 3rd parties.

Disciplinary Hearings
    Students have the right to hear the charges or rule violations, they have the right to know the supporting evidence, they can present their side of the story before a decision is made.
    Students have the right to appeal and have their case reviewed by an impartial administrator.
