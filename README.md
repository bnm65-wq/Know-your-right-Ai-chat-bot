# Know-your-right-Ai-chat-bot

Ask our AI bot to list off basic human right about human rights, consumer rights, housing rights, workplace rights, or education rights

* Presentation Slides: https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1N980Qc1se4ETirjsqNO5VEV7b7vNY1xBXndB8Ip7pnI/edit?slide=id.p1#slide=id.p1

## Knowledge Areas Covered 
* Education Rights: Student protections, accommodations, Title IX, and access to eduction.
* Human Rights: Fundamental protections, civil liberties, and anti-discrimination standards.
* Workplace Rights: Wage standards, workplace safety (OSHA), anti-discrimination (EEOC), protected leave (FMLA), and union rights (NLRB)
* Consumer Rights: Protections against unfair business practices, fraud, clear discourses, and consumer privacy
* Housing Rights: Fair housing protections, tenant rights, non-discrimination in leasing, and habitability standards.

## How to Use the Chatbot
1. Access the chatbot via the provided link above.
2. Asks questions about your rights in education, housing, employment, consumer transactions, or general civil rights.
3. Review the provided response along with relevant governing laws and enforcing agencies

Note/Legal Disclaimer: this chatbot is an educational tool designed for general informational purposes only and does not constitute legal advice or formal legal representation. 



## Style Sheet & Writing Standards
* Tone: Empathetic, informative, objective, and plain-language.
* Formatting: Direct headings, structured bullet points, and accurate citations of statutes and enforcing agencies.
* Accessibility: Accessible text formatting designed for broad public understanding.
